NOVABANK PERSONAL LOAN POLICY

Document Version: 1.0

1. PERSONAL LOAN OVERVIEW

NovaBank Personal Loan is an unsecured loan that can be used for
personal financial requirements such as education, medical expenses,
travel, home improvement, or other permitted purposes.

2. ELIGIBILITY

The applicant must meet the following general conditions:

- Minimum age: 21 years.
- Applicant must have a regular and verifiable source of income.
- Applicant must satisfy NovaBank's credit assessment.
- Final eligibility depends on income, credit profile, existing
  obligations, and other applicable bank criteria.

3. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

The following documents are generally required:

- Government-issued identity proof.
- Address proof.
- Recent salary slips or income proof.
- Last 3 months of bank statements.
- Employment proof for salaried applicants.
- Income and business documents for self-employed applicants.

Additional documents may be requested based on the applicant profile.

4. LOAN AMOUNT

Eligible customers may apply for a personal loan of up to
₹10,00,000, subject to income, credit profile, repayment capacity,
and applicable NovaBank policy.

5. LOAN TENURE

Personal loans are generally available for a tenure between
1 year and 7 years, subject to eligibility.

6. INTEREST RATE

Personal loan interest rates start from 10.5% per annum.
The applicable interest rate may vary based on the applicant's
credit profile, income, loan amount, tenure, and applicable policy.

7. PROCESSING FEE

The processing fee is up to 2% of the sanctioned loan amount,
subject to applicable terms and conditions.

training demonstration. Actual banking products may have different
terms, conditions, eligibility criteria, interest rates, and fees.

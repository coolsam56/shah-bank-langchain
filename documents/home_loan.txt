NOVABANK HOME LOAN POLICY

Document Version: 1.0

1. HOME LOAN OVERVIEW

NovaBank provides home loans to eligible customers for purchasing,
constructing, or renovating residential property.

2. ELIGIBILITY

The applicant must meet the following general conditions:

- Minimum age: 21 years.
- Applicant must have a stable source of income.
- Applicant must satisfy NovaBank's credit and repayment assessment.
- Final eligibility is subject to verification of the applicant's
  financial and employment information.

3. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

The following documents are generally required:

- Government-issued identity proof.
- Address proof.
- Income proof.
- Last 6 months of bank statements.
- Employment proof for salaried applicants.
- Business and income documents for self-employed applicants.
- Property-related documents.

Additional documents may be requested depending on the applicant
profile and property type.

4. LOAN TENURE

Home loans are generally available for a tenure between 5 years
and 25 years, subject to eligibility.

5. INTEREST RATE

NovaBank home loan interest rates start from 8.5% per annum.
The applicable rate may vary based on the applicant's profile,
loan amount, tenure, and applicable bank policy.

6. PROCESSING FEE

The standard processing fee is 0.5% of the sanctioned loan amount,
subject to applicable terms and conditions.

7. IMPORTANT NOTE

The information in this document is for the AI Banking Assistant
training demonstration. Actual banking products may have different
terms, conditions, eligibility criteria, interest rates, and fees.

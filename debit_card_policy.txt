NOVABANK DEBIT CARD POLICY

Document Version: 1.0

1. DEBIT CARD OVERVIEW

NovaBank debit cards allow customers to access their eligible
bank accounts through ATM withdrawals, point-of-sale transactions,
and supported online transactions.

2. HOW TO BLOCK A DEBIT CARD

If a customer suspects that their debit card has been lost,
stolen, or compromised, they should block the card immediately.

Customers can block their debit card through any of the following
supported methods:

- NovaBank mobile banking application.
- NovaBank internet banking.
- Calling the official NovaBank customer support number.
- Visiting a NovaBank branch.

Customers should verify their identity before requesting card
blocking through customer support or branch channels.

3. WHAT TO DO AFTER BLOCKING

After a debit card has been blocked, customers should request a
replacement card if they require continued debit card access.

The replacement card may have a different card number and
expiration date.

4. LOST OR STOLEN CARD

Customers should report a lost or stolen card immediately.
NovaBank is not responsible for delays in reporting a lost or
stolen card, subject to applicable terms and conditions.

5. ATM PIN

Customers should never share their ATM PIN, card PIN, OTP,
internet banking password, or mobile banking credentials with
anyone.

NovaBank employees will not ask customers to disclose their
confidential PIN or OTP.

6. SUSPICIOUS TRANSACTIONS

If a customer notices an unauthorized or suspicious transaction,
they should immediately contact NovaBank through an official
banking channel and report the transaction.

7. IMPORTANT NOTE

The information in this document is for the AI Banking Assistant
training demonstration. Customers should use only official
NovaBank channels for actual banking services.

NOVABANK BANKING FAQ

Document Version: 1.0


1. WHAT IS EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment.

It is the fixed or scheduled amount a borrower pays periodically
towards a loan. An EMI generally consists of both principal and
interest components.

The actual EMI depends on the loan amount, interest rate, and loan
tenure.


2. WHAT HAPPENS IF I MISS AN EMI?

If a customer misses a scheduled EMI payment, applicable late
payment charges or other consequences may apply according to the
loan agreement and NovaBank's applicable policy.

Repeated missed payments may negatively affect the customer's
credit profile and may result in additional collection or recovery
actions according to applicable terms.

Customers who expect difficulty making a payment should contact
NovaBank through an official channel as soon as possible.


3. WHAT IS MINIMUM BALANCE?

The minimum balance requirement depends on the type of NovaBank

Customers should refer to the specific account terms and
conditions for the applicable minimum balance requirement.

7. WHAT IS A PERSONAL LOAN?

A personal loan is an unsecured loan that can be used for permitted
personal financial requirements.

NovaBank personal loan eligibility, interest rates, loan amounts,
and tenure depend on applicable policy and customer profile.


8. WHAT IS A HOME LOAN?

A home loan is a loan provided for eligible residential property
purposes such as purchasing, constructing, or renovating a home.

Eligibility and applicable terms depend on the customer's profile
and NovaBank policy.


9. HOW CAN I REPORT AN UNAUTHORIZED TRANSACTION?

Customers should immediately contact NovaBank through an official
banking channel and report the unauthorized transaction.

Customers should never share their OTP, PIN, password, or other
confidential banking credentials with anyone.
